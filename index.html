Why Baller

Built for the grind

Baller started in 2025 to give North American Siege teams well-run events with real stakes, from established rosters down to five-stacks entering their first bracket.

01

Real Prize Pools

Every Baller event has had a cash prize, from $400 cups to a $1,000 invitational. Results are tracked on Liquipedia.

02

Live Broadcasts

Featured matches are cast live with a full crew of commentators, observers and producers, including partner streams on SiegeDoubleShot.

Run Right

Dedicated admins, clear formats, map veto and brackets hosted on OPL. Teams know what they're signing up for and when they play.
